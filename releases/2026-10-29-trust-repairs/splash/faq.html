Frequently asked questions

Straight answers.

What VAIRIFY is, what it costs, and what happens to your identity.

What is V.A.I.?

V.A.I. stands for Verified Anonymous Identity. It shows that a real, verified user is behind an account without revealing who that user is.

Your V.A.I. is issued by ChainPass. ChainPass verifies. VAIRIFY checks. Your legal identity never reaches VAIRIFY.

Are there different levels of V.A.I.?

The current enrollment offering is ChainPass VAI Pro. There is no level selector or other offering to choose here.

What does reserving my tier actually do?

Reserving saves your email address and holds your place in a Community Preview tier. We send a confirmation with your reservation code.

It is not an account, and it does not verify anything. The Community Preview is not open yet. When it opens you register (username, email and phone). Live ID verification and your ChainPass V.A.I. come later, when live verification is turned on. Registering and verifying are separate steps from reserving.

What is Advanced Protection?

Verification establishes that a user is the one verified behind a V.A.I. Advanced Protection is what VAIRIFY builds around that verification: screening, declarations, agreements, verified encounter history, and the safety protocols designed to protect users before, during, and after an encounter.

Verification tells VAIRIFY the right user is there. Advanced Protection determines what VAIRIFY can do with that certainty to protect its users.

Why does the Community Preview include everything except live ID verification?

A typical application can launch as an MVP, correct the bugs, and keep going. VAIRIFY cannot. It includes safety features our users will depend on for protection during real encounters.

VAIRIFY is also complicated. It brings together GPS, KYC, SMS, biometric verification, emergency alerts, and other technologies that all have to work together correctly.

Every application, especially one as large as VAIRIFY, will have bugs or issues. The Community Preview gives us the opportunity to find and correct them with the community before live KYC verification is turned on.

This is the time to find any issues, not later when someone is depending on VAIRIFY during a real encounter.

How does VAIRIFY keep its safety features free?

V.A.I. was originally developed by VAIRIFY and licensed to ChainPass when the identity technology and community platform became separate companies.

When a ChainPass V.A.I. enrollment is completed, 10% of the enrollment fee goes to the referring individual, when there is one; 10% goes to the platform that initiated the enrollment; and 10% goes to VAIRIFY.

The referral share encourages individuals to introduce V.A.I. to others. The platform share turns verification into a source of revenue instead of another expense. VAIRIFY’s share supports the infrastructure needed to keep its safety features free and available over the long term. It also allows VAIRIFY to benefit from the growth of V.A.I. as it is adopted by other platforms.

One active V.A.I. is designed to work across every participating platform, so its holder does not have to start over, pay again, or complete a different verification process for each one. Platform histories remain separate and do not travel with the V.A.I.

These percentages apply only to the ChainPass V.A.I. enrollment fee. They are never connected to an encounter, booking, service, or transaction between users.

What is the one-time 48-hour courtesy for a V.A.I.?

When VAIRIFY is the platform that brings you to ChainPass, VAIRIFY sponsors one 48-hour courtesy for your ChainPass V.A.I. VAIRIFY pays for this one-time courtesy. You owe nothing.

ChainPass hosts the courtesy page, the payment and the V.A.I. itself. VAIRIFY is the sponsor. They are separate companies with separate roles.

The courtesy is once, ever, per user: not once per platform and not once per year. Joining another platform does not reset it.

The 48 hours start when you activate the courtesy, and they do not restart. Your annual V.A.I. term begins on the day the credential is issued, not the day you later pay. If you come back six months later, you have six months left, not a new year.

The 48 hours is not a loan, bill, or payment obligation. If you do not return and pay, you owe nothing, and VAIRIFY absorbs the cost of the verification, screening, declarations, and other ChainPass services used to create that V.A.I.

If the 48 hours pass without payment, your V.A.I. stops working and reads “Not active.” The next time you try to use it on any platform, you are taken to payment. Nothing is deleted and you are not banned. ChainPass tells both you and the platform that the credential is in that state.

The courtesy exists for one reason: a VAIRIFY user should not have to accept an unverified encounter simply because the other party does not already have a V.A.I.

This 48 hours is a payment courtesy. It is a different thing from the 48-hour TruRevu review window that follows a verified meeting. Live ID verification is not switched on yet, so the courtesy is not available during the Community Preview.

What is ChainPass?

ChainPass is the technology behind V.A.I. It performs identity verification (KYC) through an external verification provider and issues your V.A.I. using a zero-knowledge architecture.

Your government ID is handled by the verification provider. ChainPass retains the verification photograph, agreement records, recovery data and enrollment contact channel. VAIRIFY receives assurance that its requirements have been met, without receiving the government ID or legal identity.

Which providers can verify my ID and run the screening?

Live ID verification and background screening are run through licensed providers that ChainPass uses. Live ID verification is not switched on yet, and none of them is used during the Community Preview.

What is the background screening?

While your V.A.I. is created, ChainPass administers background screening. VAIRIFY receives a limited result, not a record of your history.

The screening reflects the records its provider searches. It is not a guarantee about any user, and it is separate from the Law Enforcement Declaration. Live screening is not switched on during the Community Preview.

Does VAIRIFY ever see my legal identity?

No. Your government ID is checked by an external verification provider. VAIRIFY never receives your legal identity, ChainPass administers identity custody and any applicable disclosure requirements; this is not a promise of exemption from lawful requests.

What is the Law Enforcement Declaration?

During V.A.I. creation, every user responds to a declaration concerning affiliation with law enforcement.

“I represent and warrant to ChainPass that my response is complete and accurate. I understand that ChainPass relies on this representation when issuing and maintaining my V.A.I. A knowingly false response constitutes a material breach and may result in action under the ChainPass Terms and any remedies available under applicable law.”

Why does ChainPass administer and record the Law Enforcement Declaration?

ChainPass is the independent third party that creates the V.A.I. It records the user’s response at the time of creation and binds that response to the verified user behind the V.A.I. VAIRIFY receives the declaration status, not the user’s legal identity.

How do the Law Enforcement Declaration and Mutual Consent Contract work together?

They cover different things. The Law Enforcement Declaration is recorded by ChainPass when the V.A.I. is created. The Mutual Consent Contract is accepted through VAIRIFY at the time of a specific encounter.

They create two independent records, made at two different times for two different entities.

Are the declarations and agreements signed and biometrically confirmed?

Yes. Every declaration and agreement is signed, biometrically confirmed as coming from the verified individual behind the V.A.I., time-stamped, and kept on file by ChainPass. The record establishes who signed, what they signed, and when they signed it.

Can VAIRIFY retrieve my identity information?

VAIRIFY does not possess your KYC documents or legal identity during ordinary operation. It retains the original KYC session key.

If retrieval is authorized, VAIRIFY submits that key to ChainPass. ChainPass then works with the KYC provider to retrieve the information tied to the original verification.

Who can authorize the identity-retrieval process?

The Founding Council must authorize it. No founder, administrator, or staff member can make that decision alone. The Founding Council is made up of community peers and serves as the community’s conscience.

Is VAIRIFY designed to help users evade the law?

No. If a serious crime occurred during a verified encounter and a VAIRIFY user was confirmed as a suspect, the Founding Council could authorize the identity-retrieval process through ChainPass and its KYC provider.

The ultimate arbiter within VAIRIFY is the Founding Council. They are community peers, and the conscience of the community.

VAIRIFY Connect ($19.99/mo) and VAIRIFY Business ($29.99/mo) add connection, social, convenience, and business features. Safety is never behind a price.

What are the nine shields?

The protection system VAIRIFY built on top of V.A.I.:

TruRevu: a history built only from verified encounters.

VAI-CHECK: biometric confirmation on every request, every encounter.

Law Enforcement Declaration: a declaration of law enforcement affiliation, administered and recorded by ChainPass.

Mutual Consent Contract: a mutual agreement for each encounter, accepted and confirmed by both users.

Background screening: completed when the V.A.I. is created.

Free: the entire safety suite will always be free.

V.A.I. Pulse: local news and law information, plus community bulletins.

DateGuard: customized emergency response from those you trust.

Anonymity by Design: identity separation provided by ChainPass. Zero-knowledge architecture.
